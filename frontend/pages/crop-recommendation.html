Agronomic Recommendations & Best Practices

  • Basal Nutrition: Apply well-rotted farmyard manure (FYM) or compost at 4-5 tonnes/acre before sowing.
  • Certified Seeds: Use ICAR/State Agriculture University certified seed varieties with >85% germination rate.
  • Seed Treatment: Treat seeds with Trichoderma viride (4g/kg) or appropriate fungicide to prevent soil-borne seedling rot.
  • Irrigation Window: Schedule the first irrigation based on root initiation (e.g. CRI stage for Wheat) and verify upcoming 48-hour rainfall forecasts.
