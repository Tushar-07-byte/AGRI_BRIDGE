🌿 Home Cleaning & Sorting Guide

1. Cleaning at Home

  • Sieve harvested grain with standard 2mm mesh to remove chaff, weed seeds, and small stones.
  • Use winnowing fan or gentle wind to blow away light husk and dust.

2. Drying Protocol

Sun dry grain on clean tarpaulin for 1-2 days until moisture drops below 12%. Avoid drying directly on bare soil.

3. Sorting & Grading

Remove broken grains, shriveled kernels, and discolored seeds manually or using grading screens.
