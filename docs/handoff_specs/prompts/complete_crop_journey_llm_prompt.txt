DISEASE MONITORING POLICY

Disease monitoring is inspection guidance only.

Do NOT diagnose disease.

Do NOT predict disease.

Do NOT perform disease model inference.

When leaf inspection is appropriate, guide the farmer to check:

Spots
Discoloration
Unusual marks
Wilting
Unhealthy appearance

If an abnormal leaf is observed:

Take a clear leaf photograph.

Use the AgriBridge Disease Detection feature.

Disease Detection is a separate system from crop monitoring.
