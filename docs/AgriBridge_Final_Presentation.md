### Slide 1: Title & Problem Statement
- **Title**: AgriBridge — Autonomous Farm-to-Field Advisory & Action Orchestration Platform
- **Problem**: Smallholder farmers face fragmented advisory, unverified AI diagnoses, weather risks, and middleman price gouging.
- **Solution**: A unified, multi-signal platform with deterministic safety, 4-state task lifecycles, and a 14-language voice companion.

### Slide 2: Core Innovation — Deterministic Safety Engine
- AI vision models diagnose; **only the deterministic policy engine decides**.
- Multi-signal fusion: Soil Telemetry + Growth Stage + 15-Day Weather Forecast.
- Zero chemical spray when rain > 60% or wind > 20 km/h.

### Slide 3: Human-in-the-Loop Field Escalation
- Borderline confidence (< 65%) automatically triggers agricultural officer review.
- Prevents catastrophic misdiagnoses while maintaining autonomous speed.

### Slide 4: Kisan Saathi Voice Companion
- Real-time speech interaction across 14 Indian languages + Hinglish.
- Context-aware UI navigation: guides farmers step-by-step through the platform.
- Zero-hallucination guardrails: strictly ICAR-grounded.

### Slide 5: Live Marketplace & Direct Bidding
- Farmers list harvests with live Mandi price transparency.
- Buyers submit competitive bids above MSP floors.

### Slide 6: Engineering Rigor & Validation
